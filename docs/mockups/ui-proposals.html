UI proposals, groups A to G

Before and after for every visual change proposed in docs/ui-review.md, each in Vellum's dark and light themes side by side. "Before" copies today's CSS. Where there's a real choice the options are stacked, with my recommendation marked. Hover and press work on the live controls; where a state is shown frozen, it's labelled. Groups H to K are wording and behaviour, so they aren't drawn here; G's two visible items are.

Updated 12 November 2026, evening. The accent is now fixed at Deep teal (#0d9488), with --accent-text for teal text (#2dd4bf in dark, Deep teal in light); every panel here uses them, and "Before" shows the app as it is now. That settles the old A1 and A2 (text on accent fills, accent-coloured text) and removes the Accent menu from D2, so those are gone from this page; docs/mockups/accent-and-icon.html has that work.

A. Layering and paper tokens

A1. One backdrop and one shadow (item 22)

Today the ordinary dialogs dim the window by 0.35 with a 20/48 shadow; the print dialog dims by 0.45 with a 12/48 shadow. After: one --scrim (0.4) and one --shadow-modal; menus keep their lighter --shadow-pop.

A2. Printable-area outline and paper colour (items 23, 24)

The print preview's printable-area line is a third red (#e05555), unrelated to the danger red; manual layout thumbnails use off-white paper (#fbfbf9). (a) a neutral blue-grey dashed line; (b) the accent at half strength. Recommend (a): it's a guide, not a warning or a selection, and it doesn't change with the accent. Thumbnails become plain white in both.

B. One button and field style

B1. Dialog footers (items 12, 30)

Print and Manual layout restyle buttons themselves: 34 px footers against 32, wider primary padding, a brightness filter on hover, and "plain" buttons that don't hover at all. Export's lone "Close" is plain where About, Properties and Shortcuts make it primary. After: the global buttons everywhere, so every footer matches.

B2. Field heights (items 19, 29)

Fields come in 28, 30, 32 and 34 px across the Export, Print, Manual layout and preset editors, and the print fields don't use the shared style. After: two heights, 32 px in dialogs and 28 px for compact rows (the print side panel, the toolbar). One radius, 6 px.

B3. Page box and zoom box (items 11, 13)

They sit side by side but differ: 24 px Inter with radius 4 against 30 px mono with radius 6, and the zoom box drops the accent focus ring. With no document, both look live next to dimmed buttons. (a) both are boxed fields, 28 px, mono, radius 6; (b) both are quiet (no border until hover or focus, as the zoom box is now). Recommend (a): it says "you can type here" without hovering. Both options dim when disabled and get the focus ring (shown frozen on the zoom box).

C. Hover, pressed and disabled states

Nothing has a pressed state, and a dozen controls don't hover where their neighbours do (item 16). Each row shows the same control at rest, hovered and pressed, frozen. After: hover is --hover (or the button's own hover), pressed is one step darker (--press), disabled is 0.45 for buttons and fields alike.

D. One selected-state treatment

Four looks for "this one is chosen": grey in the toolbar and sidebar, pale accent with bold accent text in print, grey and bold on manual sheet tabs, and nothing at all on the sidebar toggle (item 9). (a) pale accent background with teal text (--accent-text), normal weight; (b) pale accent background, normal text; (c) grey with a hairline. Bold goes in all three, since it shifts the label's width. Recommend (a): you're happy with teal text where it helps, and it marks the choice most clearly in both themes; icons in the toolbar segments turn teal the same way.

D2. Current zoom preset (item 28)

The zoom preset list uses 28 px rows and teal text for the current one, where menus use 30 px rows and a tick. After: it's a menu like the others, with a tick on the current value; its teal text can stay.

E. One close button (items 17, 43)

Four close buttons today: the document tab's (20 px, no tooltip), the dialog X (32 px), the print X (32 px, the X doesn't brighten on hover) and the manual sheet tab's plain-text "×" with no hover at all. After: one component in two sizes, the same hover and press, and a tooltip that matches its accessible name. Each row: rest, then hover.

F. Toolbar, canvas and cursor details

F1. Toolbar sizes and icons (item 20)

The sidebar toggle is 34 px next to 36 px groups, and the icons mix 18 px at a 1.4 stroke with 14 px at 1.5. After: the toggle is 36 px, and every toolbar icon is 16 px at a 1.5 stroke.

F2. Mini map in the light theme (item 21)

In light, the mini map's background and the page are both white, so the page's edge disappears (dark is fine: a pale grey map behind white pages). (a) keep the map white, outline each page; (b) give the light map a pale grey background like dark's, so the page reads the same way in both. Recommend (b): it copies the relationship that already works, with no extra line. Dark doesn't change in either.

F3. Cursors (item 25)

Hover these. The manual strip thumbnails show a grab hand at rest, against the house rule of an arrow on controls; and while Space is held for panning, the open hand leaks onto the mini map and scrollbars. After: an arrow at rest, the grabbing hand only while dragging; the map and scrollbars set their own cursor.

F4. Debug overlay and scrollbar (item 26)

The debug overlay sits over the top of the vertical canvas scrollbar. After: it moves in by the scrollbar's width.

F5. Status-bar hint position (item 27)

The scale button appears and changes width with the document, pushing the hint sideways. After: the button keeps a fixed slot, so the hint stays put. Dashed outline marks the hint. (Item 27 was runtime, not checked; this is how it would look if it does shift.)

F6. Fit page and Fit width (item 63)

They're the only text buttons on a toolbar of icons, and both are also in the zoom box's dropdown. (a) icons with tooltips, like everything else on the toolbar: a page inside corner brackets for fit page, a page between two arrows for fit width. (b) keep the words, as now. (c) drop both buttons: they stay in the zoom dropdown, and as Ctrl+0 and the new Ctrl+2. Recommend (a): it matches the rest of the toolbar and saves about 110 px; they're used often enough to keep one click away. Hover for the tooltips.

G. Toolbar behaviour you can see

G1. Automatic navigation on the toolbar (item 34)

With automatic navigation on, the toolbar shows CAD (or Document) pressed exactly as if you'd chosen it, and clicking the pressed one silently turns automatic off. (a) a small accent dot on the pressed button means "picked for you"; going back to automatic still needs the menu. (b) a third "Auto" segment: pressed when automatic, with the resolved mode shown in a hairline; one click from anywhere back to automatic. Recommend (b): it shows the state and gives the way back in the same place, so the "View menu to change" tooltip goes. Costs one more button (about 34 px).

G2. Layout order and the no-document state (item 35)

The View menu lists Continuous, Single page, Columns; the toolbar shows Single, Continuous, Columns. With no document the menu items stay enabled while the toolbar dims them. After: Single page, Continuous, Columns in both, and both disabled without a document. Shown with no document open.

Not drawn: H (names), I (error messages), J (page ranges) and K (help and README) are wording and behaviour, written up in docs/ui-review.md.
