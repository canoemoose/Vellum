Vellum icon

Traced from Gemini's large PNG. Three changes from it: the pages are solid, so they don't show through each other; the PDF badge is gone, since Vellum opens images too and a badge can't be read below 48 px; and below 32 px a small mark takes over, with the same shapes filled and bolder, because thin outlines and glow turn to mush there. The glow comes from the strokes only, in two layers: a tight bright halo and a wide soft one.

Large icon

At Windows sizes

128 to 32 px use the large drawing, 24 and 16 px the small mark. Then the 16 px icon beside a file name, as Explorer shows it.

In Vellum's title bar

The title bar's logo would become the small mark, replacing today's page glyph.
